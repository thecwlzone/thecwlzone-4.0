As noted on my [About](/about) page, I don't do blog comments. Simply put, I have neither the time nor the inclination to act as Comment Moderator, nor do I care to add to the General Digital Hysteria.

The only comments _I_ read are under [ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com) and [Postcards From Barsoom](https://barsoom.substack.com) (highly recommended BTW). The ZH comments section has a pretty bad Signal To Noise ratio, whereas the comments under John Carter's missives are almost as good as the associated article.

The [Mises Institute](https://mises.org) just posted an article about [why they don't do comments](https://mises.org/power-market/why-comments-sections-are-not-coming-back-misesorg). I agree completely. It's worth a read...
